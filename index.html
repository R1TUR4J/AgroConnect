How AgroConnect Enterprise Works

End-to-end transparent deal flow designed to protect producer margins and buyer quality.

01

Producer Lists Real Stock

Sellers capture or upload real product photos, specify quality grades, available units, and expected unit price.

02

Investor Sends Purchase Request

Buyers filter by category or district and submit a formal Purchase Request with required quantity and proposed price.

03

Seller-Controlled Negotiation

Sellers review proposals in the Negotiation Centre and can Accept, Reject, or send a Counteroffer in real time.

04

Mutual Confirmation & Dispatch

Once both parties agree, inventory is automatically deducted to prevent overselling and an official order receipt is generated.
